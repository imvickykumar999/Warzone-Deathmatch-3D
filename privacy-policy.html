Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026
Applies to: Warzone Deathmatch 3D (website, browser game, and any related mobile store listing)

This Privacy Policy explains how Warzone Deathmatch 3D (“the Game”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our game, website, or related online services at play.24x7stream.shop and associated domains (including game.24x7stream.shop).

We designed the Game to work without accounts and without selling personal information. Please read this policy carefully. By using the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

Warzone Deathmatch 3D is a free multiplayer 3D deathmatch game operated under the 24x7stream / play.24x7stream.shop brand. For privacy questions, contact us using the details in Section 11.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you play, we may process the following categories of data:

A. Information you provide

  • Display / player name. The in-game username you enter to join a match (for example a color name or custom nickname). This is shown to other players in the same session. We do not require a real name, email address, or account registration to play.
  • Optional messages you send us. If you contact us (for example via Instagram), we receive the information you choose to include in that message.

B. Information collected automatically for multiplayer play

  • Connection / network data. To connect you to a game server, technical connection details such as IP address, connection timing, and similar network metadata are processed by our servers or by a bridge you run locally. This is necessary to establish and maintain the multiplayer session.
  • Gameplay session data. While you are in a match, the Game transmits gameplay state needed for real-time multiplayer (for example player ID, position, aim/orientation, health, shots, and similar session events) to the server and to other players in that session.
  • Device and browser technical data. Standard web/app technical information may be processed by your device or hosting environment to run the Game (for example browser type, approximate device capabilities needed for graphics, and basic request logs). We do not use this to build advertising profiles.

C. Information we do not intentionally collect

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not ask for payment card details, government IDs, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or precise GPS location.
  • We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information (such as health, biometric, or financial data) for marketing or profiling.
  • The Game does not currently integrate third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs for tracking users across apps or websites.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate, host, and maintain multiplayer gameplay;
  • Display your chosen username and gameplay state to other players in the same session;
  • Prevent abuse, cheating, spam, scanning, and service disruption;
  • Diagnose connection or server issues and improve stability;
  • Respond to support or other messages you send us;
  • Comply with applicable law and enforce our rights and safety rules.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use in-game data for interest-based advertising.

4. How we share information

We may share information only as follows:

  • Other players. Your display name and live gameplay state are visible to other players in the same match so the multiplayer experience can function.
  • Service providers / hosting. Infrastructure providers that host our website, game servers, or related networking may process connection and technical data on our behalf to deliver the service (for example cloud/VPS hosting and DNS).
  • Fonts and similar content delivery. Our website may load fonts or similar assets from third-party CDNs (for example Google Fonts). Those providers may receive standard request data such as IP address when your browser fetches those assets.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or integrity of players, the Game, or the public.
  • Business transfers. If the Game or related assets are transferred (for example as part of a reorganization), information may move with that transfer under continued confidentiality expectations where applicable.

We do not share personal information with data brokers or advertising networks for their independent marketing use.

5. Data retention

  • Live session data (positions, shots, health, and similar) is used in real time for gameplay and is not kept as a long-term personal profile after the session ends, except for short-lived technical logs needed for operations or abuse prevention.
  • Server / connection logs (such as IP addresses and join/leave events) may be retained temporarily for security, debugging, and service reliability, then deleted or overwritten in the ordinary course of operations.
  • Support messages you send us are retained only as long as needed to respond and manage the conversation.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to a small multiplayer game (for example limiting exposed services and using standard network protections). No method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure. Please do not enter passwords, private personal details, or sensitive information into your in-game username or public chat-like fields.

7. Children’s privacy

The Game is a general-audience multiplayer shooter and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Parents and guardians should supervise online multiplayer play. Because usernames and gameplay activity can be seen by other players, we recommend that minors avoid using real names or other identifying details.

8. Permissions and device features (mobile / store builds)

If you install a mobile version of the Game from Google Play or another store, the app may request only the permissions needed to run gameplay and networking (for example internet access). We do not use device permissions to access your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location for advertising.

If a future update adds advertising, analytics, or other data practices, we will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, update the store listing data-safety disclosures before those practices begin.

9. International access

Our servers and hosting providers may be located in different countries than where you live. By using the Game, you understand that information may be processed in the country where our servers or providers operate, subject to applicable law.

10. Your choices and rights

  • You can play without creating an account and can choose any display name that does not reveal personal information.
  • You can stop using the Game at any time; leaving a session ends transmission of live gameplay data for that session.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using Section 11. We may need enough information to locate relevant records (for example approximate connection time), and some ephemeral gameplay data may no longer exist.
