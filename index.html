Controls

How to play

Click the arena to lock your mouse, then move, aim, and fire. Simple controls — easy to learn, hard to master.

W A S D

Move around the arena (or arrow keys)

Mouse

Aim while pointer lock is active

LMB

Fire your weapon

C

Toggle zoom / aim down sights (or hold briefly)

Space

Jump

R / E

Reload your magazine

Esc

Release the mouse cursor

R / Space / Enter

Respawn after you go down

Gameplay

What you get

Everything you need for a quick deathmatch session — built for browser play on desktop and mobile-friendly layouts.

    Real-time multiplayer

    Fight other players live over the network. See names, health, and movement updated as the match unfolds.
